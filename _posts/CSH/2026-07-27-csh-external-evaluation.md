## Week 10: Documentation of External Evaluation

**PLTW Element J** — Documentation of External Evaluation.

### Why External Evaluation Is Different from Mentor Feedback
Your mentor (Weeks 5 and 9) evaluates technical feasibility. **External evaluation** means putting the prototype in front of the people it's actually built for — target users, another class, a teacher outside CSH, a relevant club or community member — and watching how they actually use it, not how you expect them to.

### Run the External Evaluation
- Identify 3+ external evaluators who represent your target users or stakeholders.
- Give them the prototype (or a guided demo) with minimal hand-holding, and observe.
- Collect structured feedback: a short survey or interview covering usability, whether it solves their problem, and what confused them.

### Document the Findings
- Summarize findings by theme (usability, feature gaps, trust/adoption concerns), same structure as your Element B literature review.
- Update your **Multimedia Display** (portfolio, demo video, or slide deck) with real evaluator quotes/data — this becomes part of your N@tM (Week 12) presentation evidence.
- File any resulting bugs or feature requests as GitHub Issues so they're tracked, not lost in a notes doc.

### Core Deliverables
- External evaluation plan (who, how many, method).
- Raw + synthesized feedback, organized by theme.
- Multimedia display updated with evaluation evidence.
