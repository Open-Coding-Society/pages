## Week 23: Final Reflection

**PLTW Element K, Iteration 2** — Reflection on the Design Project, closing out the full Capstone.

### Final Engineering Assessment
- Write a formal engineering assessment of the finished Beta: does it meet the revised PRD? Where does it fall short, and why (technical constraint, time constraint, scope decision)?
- Reference your Week 21 comparative data directly — claims here should be backed by the numbers, not general impressions.

### Future Roadmap
- Draft a **future roadmap**: what would the next iteration (beyond this Capstone) tackle? What did you deliberately scope out that a real product would need?

### Individual + Team Reflection Across Both Iterations
- Each team member reflects on growth across the *entire* Capstone — Trimester 1 (Alpha) through Trimester 2 (Beta): technical skills gained, what you'd tell a team starting this project fresh.
- Team discussion: what changed about how you worked together between the two iterations?

### Core Deliverables
- Final engineering assessment.
- Future roadmap.
- Individual + team reflections spanning both iterations.
