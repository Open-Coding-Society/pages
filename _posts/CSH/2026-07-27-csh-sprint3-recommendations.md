## Week 12: Presentation of Designer's Recommendations

**PLTW Element L** — Presentation of Designer's Recommendations.

### Areas Needing Further Development & Testing
- Based on your Element I test data (Week 9) and Element K reflection (Week 11), list the specific areas of your prototype that are unfinished, unreliable, or untested — be concrete about what and why.

### Synthesize Expert Recommendations from Element J
- Pull the external evaluation findings from Week 10 (Element J) and turn them into specific, actionable recommendations — not just a restatement of the feedback.

### Draft Recommendations for the Next Iteration
Write your **Designer's Recommendations**: what should change in the Sprint 4 redesign, and why.
- Recommendations should be specific and evidence-backed (e.g. "switch to server-side validation — 40% of external evaluators triggered the client-side bug") — not vague ("make it better").
- This document is what your team walks into N@tM (Element M, this same week) able to defend, and what Sprint 4 (Element A, Iteration 2) starts from.

### Core Deliverables
- Areas-needing-development list, tied to Element I/K evidence.
- Synthesized recommendations from Element J external evaluation.
- Written Designer's Recommendations for the Sprint 4 redesign.
