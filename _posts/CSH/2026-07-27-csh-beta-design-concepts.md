## Week 17: Beta Design Concepts

**PLTW Element D, Iteration 2** — Design Concept Generation, Analysis, and Selection, at production fidelity.

### Generate Production-Fidelity Concepts
- Unlike Sprint 2's exploratory concepts, these should be **production-fidelity** options — real architectural or component choices you could actually ship, informed by everything you learned building and testing the Alpha.
- Directly address your highest-RPN FMEA items (Element B, Iteration 2) in at least one concept each.

### Decision Matrix v2
- Score the new concepts against your **revised PRD** (Element C, Iteration 2) — the bar is higher than Sprint 2's matrix.
- Weight FMEA risk mitigation as an explicit criterion this time.

### Select & Justify the Beta Design
- Write **My Solution (Beta)** and its justification: which concept won, and specifically how it addresses the Trimester 1 failure modes.

### Core Deliverables
- Production-fidelity design concepts.
- Decision Matrix v2 scored against the revised PRD.
- Beta design selection + justification.
