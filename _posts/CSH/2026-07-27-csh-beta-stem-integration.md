## Week 18: Production-Fidelity STEM Application

**PLTW Element E, Iteration 2** — Application of STEM Principles and Practices, at production fidelity.

### Full Sub-System Integration Plan
- Map out how every sub-system in your Beta design (frontend/backend, sensor/controller, model/pipeline, etc.) integrates with the others — interfaces, data contracts, failure boundaries.
- This is the level of detail a production engineering team would need to divide work across people without collisions.

### Updated Technical Drawings / Architecture Diagrams
- Revise your Element E (Sprint 2) diagrams to reflect the Beta design: what's new, what's removed, what's changed and why.

### Advanced STEM Principles
- Document the more advanced math/CS/engineering principles the Beta requires that the Alpha didn't — e.g. a more rigorous algorithm, a statistical validation method, a load-handling strategy.

### Core Deliverables
- Sub-system integration plan.
- Updated technical drawings / architecture diagrams.
- Advanced STEM justification write-up.
