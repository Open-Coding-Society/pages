## Week 16: Sprint 4 Retrospective & Team Realignment

### Reflect on the Redesign
As a team, discuss Weeks 13-15: the revised problem statement, the FMEA, and the revised PRD.
- Did the root-cause analysis actually change your direction, or did you end up rebuilding the same thing with different words? Be honest — this matters for Sprint 5.
- What's the single riskiest FMEA item (highest RPN) you're carrying into the Sprint 5 build?

### Reset Team Norms & Technical Ownership
- Trimester 1 revealed real information about who's strong where. Revisit your team norms and technical ownership roles from Sprint 1 and adjust them for the Beta build.
- Update your Kanban board's role/assignment structure to reflect this.

### Set Knowledge Goals for the Rest of the Iteration
- Each team member identifies one technical skill gap (surfaced during Trimester 1) they want to close during the Sprint 5 build.

### Core Deliverables
- Sprint 4 retrospective notes.
- Updated team norms / technical ownership assignments.
- Individual knowledge goals for Sprint 5.
