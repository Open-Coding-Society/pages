## Week 12: Presentation of Project Portfolio — N@tM

**PLTW Element M** — Presentation of Project Portfolio (Final Presentation to a Panel of Judges).

### Finalize the Electronic Portfolio
- Your **Electronic Portfolio** is the full, navigable record of Sprints 1-3: problem statement, literature review, PRD, decision matrix, BOM, build procedure, test data, mentor logs, external evaluation, and your Element L recommendations.
- No paper. Everything lives in your GitHub repo / portfolio site, linked and versioned.

### Build the Multimedia Display
- Prepare a **Multimedia Display**: your prototype running live if possible, plus a visual summary (poster, slides, or screen loop) of problem → process → results → recommendations.

### Night at the Museum (N@tM)
**N@tM** is your Alpha prototype defense — a showcase where your team presents like a museum exhibit to a **panel of judges** (mentors, teachers, and/or outside guests) who walk from team to team.
- Be ready to explain and defend your design decisions to someone seeing your project for the first time — this is the same muscle as the mentor and external evaluation meetings, at higher stakes.
- Judges will likely ask about your Element L recommendations directly — know what you'd do differently and why.

### Core Deliverables
- Finalized electronic portfolio (Sprints 1-3).
- Multimedia display (live demo + visual summary).
- N@tM live defense of the Alpha prototype to the judging panel.
