## Week 14: Analyze Your Own Prior Attempt

**PLTW Element B, Iteration 2** — Documentation and Analysis of Previous Solution Attempts. In Sprint 1 this meant researching *other* people's solutions; this iteration, your own Trimester 1 Alpha prototype **is** the previous solution attempt being analyzed.

### Document the Trimester 1 Prototype as a Prior Attempt
- Write up your Alpha prototype the way you'd write up any prior solution in a literature review: what it did, what its limitations were, and what evidence (Element I data, Element J feedback) shows those limitations.

### Build an FMEA Risk Matrix
Work with your mentor to build a **Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA)** for the redesign:
- List potential failure modes for the new design direction.
- Score each on **Severity**, **Occurrence**, and **Detection** (1-10 scale each).
- Multiply to get a **Risk Priority Number (RPN)** per failure mode.
- Rank failure modes by RPN — the highest-RPN items are what Sprint 5's build must specifically guard against.

### Core Deliverables
- Written analysis of your own Trimester 1 prototype as a "previous solution attempt."
- FMEA risk matrix, reviewed with your mentor, ranked by Risk Priority Number.
