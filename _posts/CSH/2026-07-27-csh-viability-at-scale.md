## Week 19: Viability at Scale

**PLTW Element F, Iteration 2** — Consideration of Design Viability, at production scale.

### Cost at Scale
- Revise your Sprint 2 BOM to reflect **at-scale** costs: what does this cost per unit or per user at realistic deployment volume, not just for your one prototype?
- For software, this means hosting/API costs at realistic usage, not free-tier assumptions.

### Regulatory & Compliance Considerations
- Identify any regulatory, privacy, accessibility, or safety standards relevant to your domain (e.g. data privacy for student data, safety standards for physical devices, accessibility for public-facing tools).
- Document how the Beta design accounts for each one — or explicitly note it as an unresolved risk.

### Revised Build-and-Test Timeline
- Lock a timeline for the Sprint 5-6 build and test that accounts for the added complexity of the Beta over the Alpha.

### Core Deliverables
- At-scale cost analysis.
- Regulatory/compliance review.
- Revised Sprint 5-6 build-and-test timeline.
