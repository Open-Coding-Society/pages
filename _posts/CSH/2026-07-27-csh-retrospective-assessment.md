## Week 4: Retrospective and Assessment

### Reflect on Sprint
As a team, discuss what went well and what didn't across Weeks 0-3: team
formation, the instructional problem, the literature review, the PRD, and
the mentor feasibility review.

### Self-Assessment
Each team member rates their own contribution and understanding of the
Sprint 1 material, and identifies one area they want to strengthen in
Sprint 2.

### Peer and Teacher Feedback
Collect structured feedback from the peer-review partner team (Week 2) and
from your teacher on the finished PRD, portfolio, and mentor agreement.

### Set Future Knowledge Goals
Write down 2-3 specific knowledge or skill goals your team is carrying into
Sprint 2 (System Architecture & MVP Construction).

### Sprint 1 Assessment Matrix

| Benchmark | Weight |
|---|---|
| Problem statement validity | 25% |
| Quantified design specifications | 25% |
| Industry research depth | 25% |
| Agile sprint setup | 25% |

Review your team's Kanban board, portfolio, and PRD against these benchmarks before moving into Sprint 2.
