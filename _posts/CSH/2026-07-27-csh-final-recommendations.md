## Week 24: Final Designer's Recommendations

**PLTW Element L, Iteration 2** — Presentation of Designer's Recommendations, closing the loop before the Capstone defense.

### Areas Needing Further Development & Testing
- Based on your Week 21 comparative testing and Week 22 UAT results, list what's still unfinished, unreliable, or untested in the Beta — be concrete.

### Synthesize Recommendations Across Both Iterations
- Pull together mentor feedback (Weeks 5 & 9), external evaluation (Weeks 10 & 22), and your Element K future roadmap (Week 23) into a single, prioritized set of recommendations.
- Distinguish between "must-fix before defense," "known limitation to disclose," and "future work beyond this Capstone."

### Core Deliverables
- Areas-needing-development list, tied to Week 21/22 evidence.
- Final, prioritized Designer's Recommendations, feeding directly into the Element M defense (this same week).
