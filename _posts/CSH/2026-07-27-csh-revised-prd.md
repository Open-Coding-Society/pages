## Week 15: Justify Revised Design Requirements

**PLTW Element C, Iteration 2** — Presentation and Justification of Solution Design Requirements, revised to industry grade.

### Revise the PRD
- Take your Sprint 1 PRD and tighten every metric to an **industry-grade** standard — the kind of spec a real engineering team would be held to, not a rough first-pass target.
- Every metric must remain quantifiable (e.g. "reduce false-negative rate from 12% to under 3%," not "improve accuracy").

### Justify Every Change
- For each revised metric, cite the evidence driving the change: an FMEA failure mode (Element B), external evaluation feedback (Element J), or mentor input.
- A metric with no cited justification is a metric to cut or reconsider.

### Peer Review
- Trade your revised PRD with another team, same as Sprint 1's peer review.
- Have them specifically check: is every metric measurable with your team's actual tools and remaining timeline? Flag anything that looks unfeasible.

### Core Deliverables
- Revised, industry-grade PRD with quantified metrics.
- Justification for each metric change, tied to FMEA/evaluation evidence.
- Peer review notes from the partner team.
