## Week 21: Edge-Case & Comparative Testing

**PLTW Elements H + I, Iteration 2** — Prototype Testing and Data Collection Plan; Testing, Data Collection, and Analysis, at production rigor.

### Edge-Case, Load & Regulatory-Compliance Testing
- Go beyond the happy-path tests from Sprint 3. Test **edge cases** (malformed input, boundary values, unusual usage patterns), **load** (what happens under realistic concurrent usage or repeated stress), and **regulatory compliance** (Element F, Week 19 concerns, verified in practice).
- Write these as explicit test cases, not ad-hoc exploration, so they're reproducible.

### Record Data Against Revised PRD Targets
- Record results against the **revised, industry-grade PRD** (Element C, Iteration 2) — this is the real bar for the Capstone defense.

### Comparative Data Analysis: Beta vs. Alpha
- Directly compare Beta results against your Sprint 3 Alpha data, metric by metric.
- This comparison — quantified improvement, not just "it's better" — is a core piece of evidence for your Sprint 6 Capstone defense.

### Core Deliverables
- Edge-case, load, and compliance test cases + results.
- Data recorded against the revised PRD.
- Beta-vs-Alpha comparative analysis.
