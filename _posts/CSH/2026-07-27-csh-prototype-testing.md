## Week 9: Prototype Testing, Data Collection & Analysis

**PLTW Elements H + I** — Prototype Testing and Data Collection Plan; Testing, Data Collection, and Analysis.

### Second Mentor Meeting: Prototype Review
Early this sprint, demo your working MVP (Element G, Week 8) to your industry mentor.
- Show it running, not just slides — mentors give sharper feedback on something they can see fail.
- Ask specifically: what would break this at real-world scale or under real-world conditions?
- Log the feedback in your Kanban board; it feeds directly into Element J next week.

### Define Testing Criteria & Procedure (Element H)
- Write explicit **Testing Criteria**: the pass/fail thresholds tied back to your Sprint 1 PRD metrics (e.g. latency < 200ms, accuracy > 90%).
- Write a repeatable **Testing Procedure**: exact steps, inputs, and environment needed to run the test the same way twice.

### Run Physical Testing & Record Data (Element I)
- Execute the testing procedure and **Record Data** — raw numbers, not summaries, so you can re-analyze later.
- Analyze the data against your testing criteria: which metrics passed, which didn't, and by how much.

### Critical Design Review → Redesign, Refine, Re-test
- Hold a **Critical Design Review (CDR)**: present your test data to your team (and mentor if possible) and decide what must change before Sprint 3 ends.
- Redesign and refine the failing areas, then **re-test** and record the updated results.

### Core Deliverables
- Logged mentor prototype-review feedback.
- Testing criteria + testing procedure documents.
- Recorded test data (before and after CDR).
- CDR notes and re-test results.
