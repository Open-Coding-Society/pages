## Week 0: Onboarding & Readiness — Home Page Game Feedback

When you first open the Open Coding Society site, you're redirected straight into a game: **CS Pathway** — an interactive way to explore the whole course, at `/cs-pathway`. Before you write any code this trimester, play it and tell us what you found.

### Play CS Pathway
- Visit the site's home page (it redirects you automatically) or go directly to `/cs-pathway`.
- Play through as far as you can — try to unlock at least one course zone.

### Draft Feedback
Take notes as you play, organized into:
- **Bugs** — anything broken, confusing, or that didn't behave the way you expected.
- **UX Friction** — places you got stuck, lost, or weren't sure what to click or do next.
- **Feature Ideas** — one thing you'd add or change to make it better.

### Open a GitHub Issue
- Create a **GitHub Issue in your own repository** documenting your feedback — one bullet per bug, friction point, and idea from above.
- Give it a clear, specific title (e.g. "CS Pathway Feedback — Unlock Threshold Unclear on CSA Zone").

### Submit
- Submit the **URL to your GitHub Issue** as this assignment's deliverable.
