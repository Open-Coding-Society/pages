## The Direction

> Four ideas guide our redesign. The website should be simpler, easier to read, more student focused, and more visual.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>Simple Navigation</strong><br>

    Reduce the number of toolbar options and group similar pages together so visitors can find information faster.

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>Less Text</strong><br>

    Replace large paragraphs with shorter sections, cards, headings, and visuals that are easier to scan.

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>Student Focused</strong><br>

    Make programs, schedules, events, and resources easier for students to access without searching through many pages.

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>More Visual</strong><br>

    Use images, program cards, impact numbers, and simple animations to show what D.A.D. does quickly.

  </div>

</div>

## What We Are Actually Going To Improve

> These are the main changes we want to make to the website.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">The Work</div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">

    <strong>Cleaner Toolbar</strong>

    <p>Reduce unnecessary navigation options and keep the most important pages easy to reach.</p>

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>Shorter Content</strong>

    <p>Break large blocks of text into smaller sections with headings, cards, lists, and visuals.</p>

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">

    <strong>Student Accounts</strong>

    <p>Create an account area where students can view their profiles, programs, activities, and useful information.</p>

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>Teacher Accounts</strong>

    <p>Create a separate account area where teachers can access information and features designed for them.</p>

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">

    <strong>Teacher Profiles</strong>

    <p>Add individual teacher pages with a photo, short biography, background, and role.</p>

  </div>

  <div class="ocs__grid-cell">

    <strong>Events Calendar</strong>

    <p>Create one simple page showing upcoming events, dates, times, locations, and descriptions.</p>
