## Core experience

> Three proven concepts from the original PVO redesign, plus the accessibility work that carries the project forward in 2026–27.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>01 · Eligibility Prescreener</strong>
        <p>A clear three-step pathway helps veterans check likely eligibility across rent, utilities, food, transportation, home repair, and emergency hardship before beginning a full request.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>02 · Volunteer Onboarding</strong>
        <p>Volunteers see role types, time expectations, and real-world impact before signing up, with a dedicated pathway for groups and a form designed to take under two minutes.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>03 · PVO Chatbot</strong>
        <p>An always-available assistant answers common questions in plain language, points users toward the right PVO resource, and hands off to a human when a request exceeds its scope.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>04 · Accessibility First</strong>
        <p>The next stage focuses on read-aloud support, adjustable text, clearer contrast and site-wide reading controls so veterans with low vision or reading difficulties can use the experience more comfortably.</p>
    </div>
</div>

## From problem to product direction

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">Why we are continuing the project</div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Current challenge</strong>
        <p>Veterans and volunteers may need to move between long pages, forms, eligibility details, document requirements, and local resources before they know what to do next. That friction can create confusion and incomplete submissions.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>2026–27 direction</strong>
        <p>Keep the strongest ideas from the original redesign, then make the overall experience easier to read, easier to navigate, and more consistent across desktop and mobile — especially for users who benefit from accessibility controls.</p>
    </div>
</div>

<br>

## Veteran support flow

> The experience is designed around the next action a veteran or volunteer needs to take, rather than making users search through the site on their own.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-4">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">One connected support pathway</div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>1 · Identify the need</strong><p>The prescreener starts with the assistance category and checks likely eligibility across six common areas of support.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>2 · Prepare</strong><p>Dynamic checklists surface the documents needed for the selected category, along with response-time expectations and urgent local resources.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>3 · Get guidance</strong><p>The chatbot answers repeat questions, links users to the prescreener, volunteer form, and community resources, and uses a human handoff when necessary.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>4 · Use it comfortably</strong><p>Accessibility tools reduce reading and visibility barriers so the support pathway remains usable for a wider range of veterans and community members.</p></div>
</div>

## Prototype foundation

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>Adaptive eligibility</strong><p>The original prescreener used smart eligibility logic that adapted to each veteran's situation and generated document requirements from the selected assistance category.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>Live resource discovery</strong><p>Google AI Search was used to surface current local resources so the experience could point veterans toward useful options beyond static page content.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>Fast volunteer matching</strong><p>Volunteer flows emphasized role fit, availability, group participation, and a low-friction sign-up experience built to preserve momentum.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>AI-assisted answers</strong><p>The PVO chatbot concept was built around PVO programs, eligibility information, and local partners, with direct resource links and human escalation.</p></div>
</div>

## 2026–27 roadmap

> The original redesign identified accessibility, donations, and event support as the next major opportunities. The new capstone carries those ideas forward with accessibility as the immediate foundation.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>Accessibility tools</strong><p>Read-aloud support, adjustable font sizes, clearer display controls, and an accessibility-first experience aligned with WCAG 2.1 AA goals.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>Donation experience</strong><p>A faster giving flow with one-time and recurring options, preset amounts, and clearer information about how donations support PVO programs.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>Golf tournament hub</strong><p>A dedicated event page for registration, sponsorship tiers, schedules, updates, leaderboard information, and a post-event recap.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>Consistent handoff</strong><p>Keep assistance, volunteer, chatbot, and community-resource pathways connected so users always know where to go next.</p></div>
</div>
