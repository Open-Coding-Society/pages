## Our Capstone: Supporting XRCare AI's Mission

Our student team is developing a website to help people discover XRCare AI's mission, technology, resources, and contacts. The features below are proposals to review with XRCare AI; its community's needs should guide what we build.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header" style="text-align: center;">Planned Features</div>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--color" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🥽 XR Experience Showcase</strong>
        <p>Interactive previews of XRCare AI's immersive experiences, with clear descriptions of what each one does.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🤖 AI Care Guide</strong>
        <p>Proposed assistant for navigating information and resources, with scope and wording to be reviewed by XRCare AI.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📝 Wellness Check-Ins</strong>
        <p>Optional self-reported check-ins on topics such as comfort, stress, and focus.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📊 Progress Dashboard</strong>
        <p>Visual trends over time, shown only to the person who enters the data.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🚨 Care Team Notifications</strong>
        <p>Proposed alerts for care staff, subject to XRCare AI's input and privacy requirements.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🔒 Privacy and Safety Center</strong>
        <p>Plain-language explanations of how data is used, stored, and protected.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>💬 Feedback Wall</strong>
        <p>Anonymous space for users to share experiences and suggestions.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📅 Demo and Appointment Scheduler</strong>
        <p>Easy sign-up for demos, consultations, and events.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📚 Resource Directory</strong>
        <p>Vetted educational, support, and getting-started resources.</p>
