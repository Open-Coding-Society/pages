## The Story of XRCare AI

XRCare AI is building an end-to-end XR-AI platform for healthcare, designed to serve as a Point-of-Care Co-pilot™ for clinical teams, pharmacies, and care team trainers. The platform is developed by Plerion AI, Inc. and led by an executive team with backgrounds in engineering, clinical practice, and healthcare technology. Its goal is to streamline documentation, accelerate workflows, and improve accuracy where care is delivered.

Behind that technology are nurses, pharmacists, clinicians, and trainers who want less administrative burden and more time with patients. This capstone begins with their needs and XRCare AI's work: helping visitors understand the platform, see how it supports clinical teams, and connect with the people behind it.

[Visit xrcare.ai](https://xrcare.ai/)

## Our Capstone: Supporting XRCare AI's Mission

Our student team is developing a website to help clinical teams, pharmacies, and educators discover XRCare AI's mission, solutions, team, and contacts. The features below are proposals to review with XRCare AI; its users' needs should guide what we build.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header" style="text-align: center;">Planned Features</div>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--color" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🩺 Point-of-Care Co-pilot Overview</strong>
        <p>A clear introduction to how the Co-pilot supports documentation, workflows, and accuracy.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🥽 XR Training Showcase</strong>
        <p>Previews of XR simulations for onboarding in the ED, med-surg, long-term care, and home health.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🛡️ Zero-Risk Skill Practice</strong>
        <p>Walkthroughs of how clinicians can practice procedures before working with real patients.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📋 Reduced Admin Burden</strong>
        <p>Explainers on AI-assisted charting, coding, and reporting that free nurses for patient care.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>💊 Pharmacy Solutions Page</strong>
        <p>A dedicated section for pharmacy teams and care team trainers.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>👥 Team and Advisors Directory</strong>

        <strong>🔒 Trust and Compliance Center</strong>
