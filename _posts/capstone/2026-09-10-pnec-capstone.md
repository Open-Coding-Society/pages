> PNEC is a preparedness platform built for a real community organization in Poway. The previous team turned a static page into a working platform with Risk Watch, a Helper Bot, and neighborhood mapping — our job is to extend it with tools that turn information into action.

        <p>This capstone project aims to extend the Poway Neighborhood Emergency Corps platform with personalized checklists, quizzes, volunteer coordination, and multilingual support, turning a preparedness resource into a preparedness action tool.</p>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">Live Features vs What We're Adding</div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Risk Watch</strong>
        <p>Live wildfire, flood, and extreme-heat risk for Poway, pulled from weather and air-quality data.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-green fill small" href="https://pnec.opencodingsociety.com/">
            View Live Site
        </a>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Family/Household Checklist</strong>
        <p>A saved, personalized 72-hour kit and evacuation checklist tied to each resident's account, with progress tracking.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Helper Bot</strong>
        <p>A chatbot grounded in PNEC's FAQ content that answers preparedness questions like what belongs in a 72-hour kit.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-green fill small" href="https://pnec.opencodingsociety.com/">
            View Live Site
        </a>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Interactive Preparedness Quiz</strong>
        <p>8–10 questions like "Do you know your evacuation route?" ending in a preparedness score and a to-do list.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Find Your Neighborhood</strong>
        <p>Interactive map covering 60+ Poway neighborhoods, each linked to a block coordinator, fire station, and evacuation route.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-green fill small" href="https://pnec.opencodingsociety.com/">
            View Live Site
        </a>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Volunteer Shift/Task Board</strong>
        <p>Coordinators post needs (check-ins, supply drives, CERT training) and residents claim slots.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Member Accounts & Roles</strong>
        <p>Separate logins and dashboards for residents, coordinators, staff, and admins.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-green fill small" href="https://pnec.opencodingsociety.com/">
            View Live Site
        </a>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Post-Disaster Check-In Board</strong>
        <p>Residents mark themselves "safe" or "need help" by neighborhood, visible to their coordinator.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Live Theme Editor</strong>
        <p>Lets non-technical volunteers request a content change in plain English and have it committed automatically.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-green fill small" href="https://pnec.opencodingsociety.com/">
            View Live Site
        </a>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Multilingual Support</strong>
        <p>Auto-translate or offer Risk Watch, evacuation info, and the Helper Bot in Spanish and other locally common languages.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Donation Drive & Goal Tracker</strong>
        <p>PNEC lists current needs (flashlights, water, first-aid kits) and shows progress toward a goal for donors and local businesses.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>"Report a Hazard" Form</strong>
        <p>Residents flag a downed tree, blocked route, or broken hydrant, routed to the relevant coordinator or admin dashboard.</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="#">
            Planned Feature
        </a>
    </div>
</div>
