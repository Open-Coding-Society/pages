> Friends of Poway Seniors is a 501(c)(3) supporting seniors and marginalized communities in Poway. The 2025–2026 team rebuilt the site with simplified navigation, an AI chatbot, interactive Bingo, an ML event predictor, and volunteer signup. We start from that build: fix what stops an older adult from using the site today, then add three systems across the year that give people a reason to come back. **Team Triple T** — Chetan Tiduwar, Aaditya Prem, Kashyap Tubati.

## Immediate Changes

Shippable in the first two weeks, before the bigger build starts.

<div style="overflow-x: auto;" markdown="1">

| Area | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Bingo status banner | "API Ready · Bingo Volunteer API is running!" shown to every visitor | Debug message removed; page opens on the game and the volunteer form |
| Text and tap targets | Standard sizes for an audience of older adults | Larger base font and buttons, verified at 200% browser zoom |
| Chat assistant | Unlabeled floating icon, mouse only | Visible label, keyboard reachable, states what it helps with |
| Volunteer signup | No explanation of the commitment, no confirmation after submit | Plain-language explainer above the form and a clear confirmation |
| Events pages | Visitor must open the predictor to learn what's happening | Real upcoming dates listed on the page |

</div>

## The Year Ahead

Three systems, one owner each, built across four quarters.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">Three Systems, One Owner Each</div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Scam Defense Trainer</strong>
        <p>Paired scenarios — a real message beside a scam version of the same thing. The player decides whether to trust it, then taps the part that gave it away. Every round names the red flag, and scam types a player keeps missing come back more often.</p>
        <p><em>Q1 · Chetan</em></p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Digital and Multiplayer Bingo</strong>
        <p>A large-print solo game with a speaking auto-caller, then multiplayer: room codes, one shared caller keeping every board in sync, and reconnection without losing your card. Connected to the real weekly games at Mickey Cafagna Community Center.</p>
        <p><em>Q2–Q3 · Kashyap</em></p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Movement Games and Accessibility</strong>
        <p>Seated exercises turned into scored games using only a webcam — head-nod and reach-and-tap, tracking range and repetitions. Plus read-aloud mode and accessibility settings that persist between visits.</p>
        <p><em>Q4 · Aaditya</em></p>
    </div>
</div>

> **Privacy.** All webcam processing happens in the browser. No video is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere, and the site says so before the camera turns on. Exercises are seated by default with a stop button always visible.

## Work Distribution

Each of us owns one system for the year, and one algorithmic problem inside it.

<div style="overflow-x: auto;" markdown="1">

| Person | System | Algorithmic feature |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| Chetan Tiduwar — Scrum Master/Developer | Scam Defense Trainer | Adaptive difficulty — tracking missed scam types and resurfacing them on a spaced schedule |
| Kashyap Tubati — Technologist/Developer | Bingo, solo then multiplayer | Real-time state sync — one shared caller, every board consistent, reconnection without data loss |
| Aaditya Prem — Technologist/Developer | Movement games, accessibility layer | Head-movement detection — turning webcam face-landmark coordinates into counted, scored repetitions |

</div>

Each phase ends with something that works on its own rather than a piece waiting on the next quarter, and we test with real users before moving on.
