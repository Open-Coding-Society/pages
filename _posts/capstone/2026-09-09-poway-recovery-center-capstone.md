## The Story of Poway Recovery Center

Poway Recovery Center began in 1983 as the Poway Alano Club, when 25 members of Alcoholics Anonymous created a meeting space on Garden Road. Their goal was a lasting home for recovery groups. After losing its longtime lease in 2016, the community helped establish a new home on Poway Road in 2018. In 2023, the organization became Poway Recovery Center, welcoming a wider range of recovery groups and resources.

Behind that history are people seeking support and loved ones looking for connection. This capstone begins with their experiences and the center's work: helping people find a welcoming community, relevant resources, and opportunities to meet.

[Read the center's history](https://powayrecoverycenter.org/about/) · [Visit powayrecoverycenter.org](https://powayrecoverycenter.org/)

## Our Capstone: Supporting the Center's Mission

Our student team is developing a website to help people discover Poway Recovery Center's mission, meetings, resources, and contacts. The features below are proposals to review with the center; its community's needs should guide what we build.

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header" style="text-align: center;">Planned Features</div>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--color" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🎵 Mood Room</strong>
        <p>Mood check-ins paired with therapeutic music.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🤖 AI Recovery Bot</strong>
        <p>Proposed resource guidance, with scope and wording to be reviewed by the center.</p>
