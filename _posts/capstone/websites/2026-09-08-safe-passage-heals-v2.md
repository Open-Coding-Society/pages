## Core experience

> Four connected parts of the Safe Passage Heals experience, from managing community information to exploring the recovery process.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card">

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>01 · Manage media</strong>
        <p>
            Organize project media and information through tools designed
            to make community content easier to maintain and revisit.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>02 · Explore events</strong>
        <p>
            Present community events and updates in a centralized
            experience so users can discover relevant information.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>03 · Recovery simulation</strong>
        <p>
            Explore an interactive simulation representing stages of
            the recovery process and the decisions someone may encounter.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>04 · Revisit information</strong>
        <p>
            Connect media, events, and recovery information into one
            experience that users can return to as they explore the site.
        </p>
    </div>

</div>

## From problem to product direction

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2">

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">

    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">

## System flow

> A connected view of how the main parts of the project fit together.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-4">

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">
        One connected project workflow
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>1 · User interface</strong>
        <p>
            The frontend provides the pages and interactive experiences
            users interact with.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>2 · Media + events</strong>
        <p>
            Community media and event information are organized into
            accessible sections of the application.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>3 · Backend</strong>
        <p>
            Backend services are planned to support project data and
            management features as development continues.
        </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>4 · Recovery experience</strong>
        <p>
            The interactive recovery simulation connects the educational
            experience to the rest of the project.
        </p>
    </div>

</div>
