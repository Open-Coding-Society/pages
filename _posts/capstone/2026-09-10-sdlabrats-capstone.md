## The Direction

> Four things guide every change we make. The site should feel fluid, look sharper, get out of the visitor's way, and sit on a structure that was planned rather than grown.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--card" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Fluid</strong><br>
        Layout that flexes smoothly at every width, with real space between sections and quiet motion that never makes anyone wait to read.
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Better CSS and HTML</strong><br>
        One shared style layer, a real type scale, content grouped into cards, and clean semantic markup so every page has a proper outline.
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Less Cluttered Navigation</strong><br>
        Five items at the top, nothing appearing twice under two names, and Enroll kept as the loudest thing in the bar.
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>New Architecture</strong><br>
        Every piece of content gets exactly one home, which is the thing that stops the repeated blocks from coming back.
    </div>
</div>

## What We Are Actually Going To Improve

> The short list. Every one of these is something we can point at when it is finished.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">The Work</div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>One Home Each</strong>
        <p>Give every block of content a single owning page and replace the copies with links.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Five Item Menu</strong>
        <p>Cut the bar down and remove every entry that lands on a page another entry already reaches.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>One Program Layout</strong>
        <p>Build the program page once and use it four times so parents can actually compare.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Shape The Text</strong>
        <p>Break the long plain stretches into sections with headings, cards, and room to breathe.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>One Style Layer</strong>
        <p>Write the styles once and apply them everywhere, instead of each page making its own choices.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Money At A Glance</strong>
        <p>Put pricing, schedule, scholarships, and charter funding where a parent can read them quickly.</p>
    </div>
</div>
