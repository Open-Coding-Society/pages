<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">Key Feature Breakdown &amp; Ideation</div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>1. Streamlined Navigation</strong>
        <p>Reduce toolbar clutter and prioritize only the most important pages so users find what they need quickly.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>2. Less Text, More Clarity</strong>
        <p>Cut long blocks of copy into shorter sections, bullet points, and visuals so the site reads faster.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>3. Student &amp; Teacher Accounts</strong>
        <p>Separate login/account areas so each group can manage a profile and access personalized features.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>4. Teacher "About" Pages</strong>
        <p>Individual bio, photo, and role pages so students and parents can get to know the staff.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>5. Easier Student Experience</strong>
        <p>Simpler navigation and fewer steps to register, view activities, and stay engaged.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>6. Events / Calendar Page</strong>
        <p>A clean calendar with dates, times, locations, and short descriptions of programs and activities.</p>
    </div>
</div>
