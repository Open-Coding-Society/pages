> **San Diego Rescue Mission — Website Improvement.** A redesigned site that helps residents in crisis find the right service fast, shows donors exactly what their gift provides, and gives volunteers a clear view of open opportunities. **Status: In Planning.** Team: Aarav Nadar (Scrum Master), Ryden Bercovitch (Technologist/Developer).

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">The Problem</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Who they serve</strong>
<p>San Diego Rescue Mission has served the city's homeless and food-insecure community since 1955 — emergency shelter, a residential program, a children's resource center, and hunger relief.</p>
<a class="ocs__btn alert-green fill small" href="https://www.sdrescue.org/">Live Site</a>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<strong>What's wrong today</strong>
<p>The current site buries the services people actually need under walls of static text, which makes it hardest to use for the people who need it most: someone in crisis.</p>
<a class="ocs__btn alert-yellow fill small" href="https://www.sdrescue.org/get-help-now/">Get Help Now</a>
</div>
</div>

## Key Features

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">Five Changes</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Filterable Get Help Now</strong>
<p>Residents filter available services by need and location instead of reading one long static list — filter by need (shelter, meals, resources) and by location.</p>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Donation Impact Calculator</strong>
<p>Donors see exactly what any dollar amount provides, for one-time or monthly gifts.</p>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Filterable Volunteer Opportunities</strong>
<p>Volunteers filter by availability and see live spots remaining per slot.</p>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Site-Wide Search</strong>
<p>Any program page is reachable by typing its name into a header search box.</p>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Accessibility &amp; Mobile Audit</strong>
<p>Every page checked for alt text, contrast, and correct rendering on a 375px mobile screen.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Impact

<div style="overflow-x: auto;" markdown="1">

| Change | What it does for the user |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Filterable Get Help Now | Cuts the time it takes someone in crisis to find the right service |
| Donation Impact Calculator | Shows donors the concrete outcome of their gift |
| Volunteer slot filtering | Gives volunteers real-time visibility into open slots |
| Site-wide search | Makes every program reachable without navigating the full menu |
| Accessibility & mobile audit | Ensures the site works on any device, not just desktop |

</div>

## Scope

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header">What We Are and Aren't Building</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>What We Built</strong>
<p>A redesign of the Get Help Now, Ways to Give, and Volunteer pages, plus site-wide search, so each user group can find what they need in a few clicks.</p>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell">
<strong>Out of Scope</strong>
<p>We are not rebuilding pages that already work. The redesign is limited to the three pages above plus search.</p>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
<strong>Why It Matters</strong>
<p>The people least able to fight a confusing website are the ones this site exists to serve.</p>
</div>
</div>
