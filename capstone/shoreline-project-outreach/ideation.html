— 01

What do they do?

Shoreline Community Services (shorelinecs.org) is a San Diego nonprofit focused on the unmet needs of unsheltered individuals and families in the Central Beach Area — Pacific Beach, Mission Beach, and La Jolla.

The Compass Station

A drop-in resource center for unhoused individuals to receive services needed to begin their journey off the street.

Volunteer Community Outreach

A team of trained volunteers that meet unsheltered individuals where they are, building relationships, trust, and support.

Medical Respite Hotel Program

For medically fragile individuals recovering after a hospital stay, with a safe place to heal.

Community Care Crew

A transitional employment program including paid work, case management, career counseling, and temporary workforce housing.

— 02

Why them?

We chose Shoreline Community Services because it directly addresses one of San Diego's most pressing social challenges — unsheltered homelessness in the Central Beach Area — with a practical, multi-layered approach rather than a passive one.

This combination — meeting the needs of housing and employment — is exactly the type of impact we want to support. Shoreline is influential while also being locally rooted, and they already work with many partners around San Diego. So instead of our effort standing alone with limited effect, it adds onto a cause that's already part of something bigger.

Their three-part approach

  • Volunteer Community Outreach
  • Medical Respite Hotel Programs
  • Compass Stations

Because they already touch housing, health, and employment, our features only need to plug into an existing structure — not invent a new one.

— 03

What we want to add to their site

We brainstormed six features, then paired them into two combined ideas that reinforce each other. These are proposals for what could be built — not built yet.

Combined idea — volunteering

Homelessness Hotspot Map

Selecting "volunteer" shows a map of the most concentrated homeless areas and the places most in need of volunteers.

  • Directs volunteers to places of greater need, not just the nearest sign-up center
  • Could reduce manual coordination — the system directs volunteers instead of a person doing it by phone or email
+

Volunteer Shift Calendar

Volunteers see which shift slots are already taken instead of being contacted individually by a team member.

  • Fewer open spots create urgency, encouraging more sign-ups
  • Removes a manual back-and-forth step for the outreach coordinator

Why pair these: a hotspot map is more useful the moment it's next to open shifts at that exact location — otherwise a volunteer sees the need but still has to go find a way to act on it.

Combined idea — giving

Donations Page

Right now, the only way to donate is through an Instagram link in their bio. A dedicated page makes it easier to contribute.

  • Framed amounts like "$50 = 1 meal + supplies" and "$200 = 1 night hotel stay" show donors what their money does
  • Concrete impact, not a random number field, encourages larger gifts
+

Needs Right Now Page

Money isn't the only thing people can give — most people who are comfortably settled can afford to donate items like toys, clothes, or food.

  • Shows which objects are most needed near a shelter
  • Guides donors on exactly how to give those items

Why pair these: Needs Right Now becomes a section within the donations page — it opens the door to people who can't give money but can still give something.

— 04

On the horizon

Two more ideas came out of the brainstorm. They're documented here but not yet prioritized for the first build.

Not yet prioritized

Service Navigation

A "Need Help?" button for anyone unhoused visiting the site — showing directions to the nearest shelter, its facilities, and outreach team contact info if they need extra support.

Not yet prioritized

Impact Dashboard

A dashboard of stats over a selectable time range (last 30 days, last week, etc.), with a graph of how many people have been helped over the last year — showing real impact instead of just listing programs.
