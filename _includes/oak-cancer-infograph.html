Oncology and Kids · Capstone
Connecting learning, care, and community

This capstone project is designed to make cancer education and support more accessible, interactive, and welcoming. The goal is to help patients and families learn about cancer in an easier way while also creating a space for connection, play, and encouragement.

Experience: learn → connect → explore → play → feel supported

Project focus: educational modules, community support, mood check-ins, interactive games, reading level filters, risk education, and a kid-friendly learning experience.

From problem to product direction

Why we are building it
Current challenge

Many cancer resources are difficult to navigate, overly text-heavy, or not designed to feel welcoming for younger patients. Kids with cancer often need a space that is both informative and emotionally supportive.

Project direction

Build a more engaging and inclusive website that combines education, community support, and interactive play in a way that feels friendly, understandable, and empowering.

Core features

Community support

Create a welcoming space for kids and families to feel connected, supported, and less isolated while navigating cancer treatment and recovery.

Daily check-in

Offer a simple daily mood check-in so users can share how they are feeling and connect with others in a supportive, low-pressure way.

Interactive games

Include puzzles, memory activities, and playful educational tools that make the experience more enjoyable and interactive.

Reading level filters

Organize information by age or reading level so kids, teens, and parents can access content that is easier to understand.

Risk calculator

Provide educational guidance through a risk-informed feature that helps users explore relevant health information in a clearer way.

Cell lab

Refresh the past cell lab concept into a more accessible, interactive learning experience that explains cancer science in a simpler format.

Body map

Create a kid-friendly body map tool that helps users better understand cancer-related health information through interaction and visual learning.

System flow

The project is designed as a connected experience where educational content, community features, and interactive tools work together.
One connected support and learning workflow
1 · User interface

Visitors access educational modules, games, check-ins, and support features through a welcoming and easy-to-navigate website.

2 · User actions

Users explore information, interact with content, participate in activities, and connect with a community designed around support and understanding.

3 · Content and tools

Educational content, risk-related guidance, and interactive features help make cancer information more helpful and approachable.

4 · Community value

Users leave with more knowledge, stronger support, and a more engaging experience that encourages learning and connection.

Technical foundation

Frontend

Jekyll, HTML, JavaScript, and accessible web design for a child-friendly learning and support platform.

Backend

Python Flask services can support data-backed information, user interactions, and future community or educational workflows.

Content

Educational modules, reading-level filters, and interactive learning features are designed to make complex information easier to understand.

Project goal

Turn the strongest ideas from earlier work into a clear, maintainable product direction focused on inclusion, education, and support.
