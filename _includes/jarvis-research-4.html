Research Area 4 · Spatial Modeling

Research 4: Tracking & Room State Model

Tracking physical assets across time, reconciling multi-camera viewpoint observations, handling occlusion, and detecting genuine room change events.

Research Question 4

How effectively can multi-camera observation fusion and temporal smoothing distinguish true physical movements and removals from transient occlusions and noise, maintaining 80%+ state accuracy?

Acceptance & Test Cases

  • Correlate object observations across sequential 10-second frames using spatial IoU and feature proximity
  • Correctly classify object states across five test scenarios: NEW, CONTINUING, MOVED, OCCLUDED, and MISSING
  • Achieve at least 80% correct object-state decisions on held-out multi-frame validation sequences
  • Prevent transient person occlusions from immediately triggering false MISSING or MOVED state alerts
  • Fuse observations from Camera 1 and Camera 2 into a unified room coordinate space without duplicate ghost entries
