Why this matters

Safety information should be easier to find before it is needed.

Our direction is a student-built interface around searchable standards, assisted discovery, personal equipment tracking, and staff-oriented management workflows.

Problem → Direction

Turn a large standards library into a clearer workflow.

The project is not trying to replace official SFI documentation. It explores how software can make that information easier to discover and organize while keeping the official standards and PDFs as the source of truth.

Current challenge

Users can face dense lists, unfamiliar specification numbers, and multiple documents when trying to determine which safety standard applies to a piece of equipment.

Project direction

Combine structured spec data, plain-language search, ML suggestions, gear tracking, and guided tools into one consistent frontend backed by a Flask API.

Core Experience

A four-step path from question to useful safety information.

01

Search standards

Browse categories or search specification records in plain language instead of relying only on exact spec numbers.

02

Describe a part

A TF-IDF + LinearSVC classifier can suggest likely specification matches from a free-text equipment description.

03

Inspect equipment

Browser-side TensorFlow.js models explore image and camera-based equipment recognition as an assistive discovery tool.

04

Track and revisit

Users can organize personal gear, revisit certification information, and ask the site chatbot questions about the available spec data.

Engineering Destination

Six connected areas we are developing and refining.

Standards Search

Structured categories, statistics, fuzzy discovery, metadata, and links back to official specification documents.

Spec Classifier

ML-assisted matching for users who know what a part does but do not yet know the relevant specification number.

Equipment Detector

A browser-first experiment using camera or uploaded images to suggest likely motorsports equipment categories.

My Gear

Personal equipment records designed to help users keep important safety gear information organized in one place.

AI Assistant

A Gemini-backed chatbot that can answer questions using compact SFI specification context supplied by the backend.

Staff Tools

Administrative workflows for users, groups, permissions, gear review, and specification maintenance are the next major integration area.

Research & Validation

We want to measure whether the experience is actually easier to use.

Validation is centered on realistic tasks rather than visual polish alone.

Discovery tests

Can a user find a relevant standard by category, keyword, typo-tolerant search, or a plain-language description of a part?

Integration tests

Do frontend flows behave consistently with the local/deployed API, authentication state, saved gear data, and classifier responses?
