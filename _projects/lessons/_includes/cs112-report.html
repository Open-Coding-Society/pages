CS112 Instruction / Examples

This report follows MiraCosta's CS112 outline, not the AP question order. AP FRQs are supporting practice within the relevant college topic; their titles retain the practice category and original exam identity. A lesson may support more than one topic without becoming an additional assignment.

Teaching stage: FRQs are taught in CSA2 and beyond. Their placement under a CS112 topic records articulation alignment, not CSA1 instruction or a change to the shared course schedule.

Early Web foundations: POJO, JPA, and APIs are introduced in CSA1 week 4, building on students' prior Python Web and API work in APCSP. These familiar project patterns provide an entry point into Java; later CSA2 and DS2 lessons deepen the related CS112 concepts.

Weeks and supplemental status come from the current lesson metadata. Drafts are authoring work, not live assignments. Coverage notes describe available material, not earned credit or demonstrated mastery.
