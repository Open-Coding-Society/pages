## Communication

1. **Individual learning journey**
   - Use personal issues or blogs to describe your learning across the sprints.
   - Bring together your Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 retrospectives and your Sprint 3 work in teaching, homework, and the game.
   - Explain your individual contribution, including when the work was completed as a team.

2. **Teaching and lesson preparation**
   - Link the team issues assigned to you and the issues or blogs that show your preparation for teaching and grading.
   - Explain what you contributed to the lesson and how you supported classmates as they learned.

3. **Homework**
   - Link your completed homework and submissions for AI evaluation, following the Assignments Evaluation checklist.
   - Make your work understandable: explain what each link demonstrates and identify any unfinished portions.
   - When you are responsible for grading homework assigned by your group, explain how you reviewed submissions and provided useful feedback.

4. **Home page, blogs, and game**
   - Be ready to demonstrate your personal website and game contributions.
   - Use your home page and blogs to highlight key accomplishments throughout the trimester. Connect those accomplishments to evidence of your own work.
