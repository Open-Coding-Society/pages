### Wayfinding Journey

Use your reflections to identify something you want to continue, something you want to change, or a question you still want to explore.

1. **Am I on track? (Temporal wayfinding)**
   - How does my progress compare with the Calendar and Announcements?
   - Do my OCS completion and burndown records reflect my actual work and the assignment timeline?
   - When I fall behind or encounter confusion, how do I respond?

2. **Am I collaborating with my peers? (Social wayfinding)**
   - How is my group participating in the classroom and communicating with the teacher?
   - How do I support my group inside and outside the classroom?
   - What feedback have I given or received, and what changed because of it?

3. **What does the evidence tell me about my learning? (Analytical wayfinding)**
   - How do I seek clarity, advocate for my learning, or mentor a classmate on homework?
   - When my group assigns homework, how do I help monitor submissions and support classmates?
   - How do I initiate or respond to chat in ways that move our work forward?
   - What do my OCS analytics reveal about my habits? What do those records not capture?
