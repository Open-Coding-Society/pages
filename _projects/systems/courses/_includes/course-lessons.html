{%- comment -%} This course's lessons for overview pages: coursePosts are the posts and pages whose front matter puts them in current_course, including shared documents; lessonsByWeek groups them by week, so a week's lessons are one lookup (lessonsByWeek | where: "name", week). plus: 0 reads "2" and 2 alike. Used by the reader overview shell and the legacy course player. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign posts = null | compact -%} {%- assign posts = posts | concat: site.posts | concat: site.pages -%} {%- assign coursePosts = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for post in posts -%} {%- if post.courses[current_course] and post.reader_redirect_url == nil -%} {%- assign coursePosts = coursePosts | push: post -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- if current_course == 'csa' -%} {%- assign coursePosts = coursePosts | sort: "title" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign lessonsByWeek = coursePosts | group_by_exp: "post", "post.courses[current_course].week | plus: 0" -%}
